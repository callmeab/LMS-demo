About Our School

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Founded in 1985, City Public School has been a cornerstone of quality education in Lahore. For over four decades, we have been nurturing young minds with a perfect blend of modern education and strong Pakistani values.

Our institution is recognized by the Punjab Board of Secondary Education and has consistently produced top-ranking students at the district and provincial levels.
